[03] DISCOVERY+ HIDE & SEEK

2021
Discovery Inc.
Illustration | Social Statics

I was given the fun challenge of designing a 'seek and find' illustration for discovery+ socials. The goal was to subtly incorporate representations of some of the wide variety of shows available on the platform, sparking curiosity and encouraging engagement. I had a great time weaving in little stories throughout the image - see if you can spot them all!
