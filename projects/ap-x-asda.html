[03] GEORGE ASDA x ANIMAL PLANET

2022
Discovery Inc. / George ASDA
Animation

In Spring 2022, Animal Planet partnered with George at ASDA to launch a sustainable children's clothing range. To support this, we created three playful sponsorship idents that brought the clothing's illustrated characters to life, from narratives derived from slogans that featured on the clothing.

{% include full-video.html video="/videos/ap-x-asda-ident-02.mp4" poster="/images/projects/ap-x-asda-ident-02-cover.jpg" %} {% include full-video.html video="/videos/ap-x-asda-ident-03.mp4" poster="/images/projects/ap-x-asda-ident-03-cover.jpg" %} {% include full-video.html video="/videos/ap-x-asda-ident-01.mp4" poster="/images/projects/ap-x-asda-ident-01-cover.jpg" %}

The quotes featured on the clothing 'Love our planet', 'Be Kind To Every Kind' and 'Protect Nature's Beauties' were used as inspiration to develop 3 short narratives, featuring the illustrated characters that appeared on the clothing. We brought these to life in a series of 3 fun and playful sponsorship idents and accompanying social statics.

This was a really fun project, where I was lucky to be able to have a lot of creative freedom. It was great to be able to get involved with finding and selecting an audio track, working with the audio engineer, voice over artist, edit assistant, and post production to really elevate the initial graphics and bring the idents to life.
